Calculation

Speed ratio is baseline time / comparison time. Values above 1.0× are faster. Average improvement uses the geometric mean of per-test speed ratios, which gives equal weight to proportional changes in fast and slow tests. Only tests that passed and have positive elapsed times in both runs are included.

Two runs are required

Add at least two entries to runs.json to compare performance.
